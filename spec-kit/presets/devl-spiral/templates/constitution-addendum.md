
## DEV|L evolution principles

### Evidence-driven maturity spiral

Every spiral turn begins with a human maturity judgment and top-level agent selection. Grade 1/Engineer establishes a minimal working chain; Grade 2/Scientist evolves an excellent defensible core solution; Grade 3/Business evolves a scalable market-ready ecosystem. Grades are descriptive, repeatable and reversible—not acceptance gates or automatic promotion.

### Existing environment first

Start from the real physical, digital, organizational and commercial environment. Examine current practice and credible reuse, standards, open, commercial and integration options before custom construction. Evaluate delivered capability through authorized real-world application; keep simulated and synthetic evidence explicitly labelled.

### Scientific integrity

Preserve domain semantics, units, time/reference basis, missing-data meaning, provenance, uncertainty, validity and reproducibility where relevant. Distinguish facts, assumptions, hypotheses, model outputs and observations. Retain negative and inconclusive evidence.

### Core before ecosystem

Preserve reusable knowledge and interfaces while evolving maturity. Keep user experience, identity, licensing, monetization and marketing policy modular around domain/scientific truth, and introduce ecosystem machinery only when justified by validated application needs.

### Single artifact authority

Persist DEV|L and Delivery context only in native `.specify/` and `specs/` artifacts. Do not create parallel maturity records, development logs, specifications, plans, tasks or session handoffs.
