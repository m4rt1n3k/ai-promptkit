
## DEV|L engineering obligations

<!-- Apply the common obligations and only the selected grade subsection. -->

### Common

- Existing-system boundaries, ownership, compatibility and reuse/integrate/build decision
- Data semantics, units, time/reference basis, missingness, provenance and uncertainty
- Observability, reproducibility, failure handling, safe rollout and rollback
- Separation of field, simulated and synthetic evidence

### Grade 1 — minimal working chain

- Real connection and representative-data path from input through decision/action to observable result
- Transparent heuristic or baseline, diagnostics and explicit manual steps
- Reusable contracts and fixtures without premature modelling or platform scope

### Grade 2 — excellent core solution

- Mechanism/model rationale, credible baselines and validity domain
- Robustness, sensitivity, failure regimes and reproducible evaluation
- Simplest architecture that protects and exposes the validated scientific core

### Grade 3 — scalable market-ready ecosystem

- Repeatable adoption, configuration, integration, operation, support and lifecycle ownership
- Coherent, accessible user experience and trustworthy presentation of claim limits
- Evidence-backed scaling, identity, licensing, monetization and marketing mechanisms isolated from domain/scientific truth
