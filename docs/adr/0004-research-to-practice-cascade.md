# ADR-0004: Research-to-practice lifecycle around DEV|L

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-07
- Amends: [ADR-0002](0002-devl-spiral.md) artifact scope outside software Delivery

## Context

The DEV|L spiral governs iterative scientific-software development but does not cover formation of a fundable idea, call-compliant project initiation, scientific publication or transfer into practice. Those stages require different authorities and durable artifacts. UCEEB project design also requires portfolio, partner and TAČR call context that a maturity-grade agent does not own.

## Decision

1. Place DEV|L at the center of the cascade Idea → Project initiation → DEV|L → Scientific dissemination and/or Business exploitation.
2. Keep Engineer, Scientist and Business as the three reversible DEV|L grade agents.
3. Add Projectant as the upstream UCEEB–CTU/TAČR project-initiation and proposal-integration lead; it is not a maturity grade or Delivery authority.
4. Make Scientist responsible for cited state-of-art input during idea formation and for leading the downstream article.
5. Make Business responsible for sourced market/practice input during idea formation and for leading downstream exploitation.
6. Make Engineer responsible for feasibility input and reusable technical evidence.
7. Let Projectant generate call-driven candidate projects from authorized UCEEB portfolio/partner data and global analogues, then assemble the human-selected proposal with specialist contributions.
8. Preserve human authority over idea selection, commitments, submission, authorship, publication, deployment and commercialization.
9. Keep native Spec Kit as the sole software-delivery artifact system. Store proposals, manuscripts and exploitation packages in their own authoritative systems and connect stages by stable, versioned evidence references.
10. Require verified connector access, source/version dates and explicit fact/inference/assumption separation; unavailable internal data cannot be inferred.

## Consequences

- The development spiral remains central and unchanged in grade semantics.
- Project applications become formally coherent without making Projectant an all-knowing agent.
- Publication and exploitation may proceed independently and can return evidence gaps to another spiral turn.
- External Teams, UCEEB and TAČR integrations are required capabilities but remain permission-bound and deployment-specific.
- The former “single artifact system” rule applies to software Delivery, not to legitimate proposal, publication or exploitation outputs.
